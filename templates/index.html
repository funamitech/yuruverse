What is YuruVerse?

One name for everything

Every service lives under one domain: funami.tech. Yui Funami's surname. Everyone using them is technically a Funami.

Home-hosted

A literal house. YuruMirror is the only one that moved out. We're not jealous or anything.

Open source

This whole thing is a hobby. The source sits on GitHub at funamitech, and patches from strangers are welcome. Namori-sensei owns Yui. We just page her at 3am.
