What is YuruVerse?

One name for everything

Your Matrix ID is @you:funami.tech. Your Mastodon handle is @you@funami.tech. Same domain as the page you're reading. YuruChat, YuruToot, YuruLemmy, YuruMC, YuruMirror: five services, one domain.

Hosted in Gyeonggi, South Korea

Everything here runs from a house in Gyeonggi, South Korea. Not a datacenter, not a startup. Only YuruMirror got promoted to Oracle Cloud in Seoul. We're not jealous or anything.

Open source

A hobby, built in public. The source sits on funamitech where anyone can read it, and patches from strangers are welcome. Yui is borrowed with love: Namori-sensei owns her, we just page her at 3am.
