What is YuruVerse?

One name for everything

Your Matrix ID is @you:funami.tech. Your Mastodon handle is @you@funami.tech. Same domain as the page you're reading right now. YuruChat, YuruToot, YuruLemmy, YuruMC, YuruMirror — five services, one domain, zero naming creativity. Yui Funami picked the prefix and we were too tired to argue. She's been on call since day one, so she gets to name things.

Hosted in Gyeonggi, South Korea

Everything here runs from a house in Gyeonggi, South Korea. Chat is deliberately kept apart from the rest, so it stays up when something else falls over. Not a datacenter, not a startup. Only YuruMirror got promoted to Oracle Cloud in Seoul. We're not jealous or anything.

Open source

A hobby, built in public. Nothing here is a product and nobody is on a roadmap; the source just sits on funamitech where anyone can read it and judge our indentation. Issues and patches from complete strangers are genuinely welcome — Yui gets lonely. She's borrowed with love, by the way: Namori-sensei owns her, we just page her at 3am.

Keep the lights (and Yui) on

We're short of money and we need YOUR HELP! Donations keep the servers running and might let us add new services. Electricity, disks, the occasional dead fan. None of it goes to rum raisin ice cream, no matter how many times Kyōko asks.
